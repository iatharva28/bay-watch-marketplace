# BAY — Product Context

## What is BAY?

BAY is a modern Swiss-inspired luxury watch maison. The brand positions itself around a single philosophy: **"Quiet Luxury. Timeless Precision."** BAY does not compete through loud marketing — it competes through craftsmanship, mechanical excellence, and design that outlives the moment.

## Brand at a glance

- **Name:** BAY (never expanded into an acronym; standalone, like Rolex or OMEGA)
- **Founded:** 1947, Le Crêt-du-Locle, Switzerland
- **Category:** Haute horlogerie / luxury mechanical watches
- **Price range:** $28,900 (Tide diver) → $2,200,000 (Zenith grand complication)
- **Production:** Series pieces + limited editions (5–50 pieces) + unique private commissions
- **Tone:** Confident, restrained, editorial. Never shouts. Never discounts.

## Target audience

- Watch collectors and connoisseurs
- Entrepreneurs, executives, investors
- Designers, architects, engineers
- People who value mechanical engineering and heritage craftsmanship
- Buyers who prefer discretion over visible logos

## Competitive set (inspiration, not imitation)

- **Rolex** — for sport-watch authority and integrated bracelets
- **Patek Philippe** — for grand complications and generational heirloom positioning
- **A. Lange & Söhne** — for movement finishing and German restraint
- **MB&F** — for bold mechanical storytelling
- **Chronoswiss** — for accessible grand complications
- **Apple** — for digital experience quality and motion language

## The digital flagship's job

The website is **not an e-commerce store**. It is an immersive digital flagship — closer to an interactive museum than a shop. Its job is to:

1. Make visitors feel they have stepped into the world of BAY (not "welcome to our site")
2. Convey the richness of a multi-million dollar maison through 12 distinct collections
3. Communicate craftsmanship, heritage, and mechanical credibility
4. Create desire for pieces that are acquired by appointment, not added to a cart
5. Route serious inquiries to private client advisors and boutiques

## Success metrics

- Time on page (long-form engagement, not bounce)
- Scroll depth (the experience is designed as a single cinematic scroll)
- Private appointment requests
- Perception of brand prestige (qualitative)

## What the site is NOT

- Not a shop — no add-to-cart, no checkout
- Not a catalog — no filters by price, no sorting
- Not a blog — the Journal is editorial, not SEO content
- Not loud — no banners, no popups, no urgency tactics

## Content universe

- **12 watch collections** across 5 families: Flagship, Grand Complications, Travel, Sport, Ultra-Thin
- **6 limited editions** (Eclipse, Lumen, Nimbus, Atlas, Polaris, Zenith)
- **6-milestone heritage timeline** (1947 → 2024)
- **4 craftsmanship pillars** (Movement, Case, Dial, Assembly)
- **8 in-house calibers** with animated movement visualizations
- **6 worldwide boutiques** (Geneva, Paris, Tokyo, New York, Singapore, Dubai)
- **4 journal entries** (long-form editorial)
